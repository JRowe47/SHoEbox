per arm/seed. A7 preserves that count, every label, mask, length, phase and RNG
draw. It changes only the registered original-record spacer bytes.

The intended coverage increase occurred. It improved the measured false-positive
behavior, but clean-context identity and version selection deteriorated.
This motivates retaining clean-spacer support while adding diversity. It does
not establish that a mixture will recover the gates.
